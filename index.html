I.

Emily Dickinson

19TH-CENTURY POETRY
Lad of Athens, faithful be
“Lad of Athens, faithful be To Thyself, And Mystery— All the rest is Perjury—”

Radical loyalty to the inner self against social hypocrisy.

II.

Jacques Lacan & Slavoj Žižek

PSYCHOANALYSIS & IDEOLOGY
Fetishistic Disavowal
تميمة الإنكار
“I know very well, yet nonetheless I act as if I do not.”

III.

William Butler Yeats

1936 · POETRY IN A DARK TIME
Lapis Lazuli (Tragic Joy)
“All things fall and are built again, And those that build them again are gay.”

Retaining creative joy, laughter, and poetry amidst civil collapse.

IV.

Sylvia Plath & Robert Frost

1963 / 1915 · THE DEFIANT SELF
The Ontology of Meaning
“I took a deep breath and listened to the old brag of my heart: I am, I am, I am.”

Facing the existential void and choosing the defiant road of living.

V.

Leonard Cohen (after Cavafy)

2001 · A SONG OF DIGNIFIED LOSS
Alexandra Leaving
“Say goodbye to the Alexandra that you are losing... as someone prepared long beforehand.”

Grieving lost political dreams and beloved cities with noble dignity rather than cynical bitterness.
