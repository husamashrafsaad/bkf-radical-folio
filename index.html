A FEW WORDS TO LIVE BY

CANON AXIOMS · § 01
AXIOM I

The Sanctuary of Lingering

ملاذ التلكؤ والتأني

Slowness in an age of hyper-extraction is not indolence; it is mutiny. To sit over boiling cardamom tea, to sketch, to daydream without profit is to reclaim the stolen hours of our human lives.

AXIOM II

Radical Tenderness

الحنان فعل مقاومة

Cruelty is the blunt weapon of the insecure despot. Tenderness—fierce, unyielding, collective care for the fragile and the steadfast—is the foundation upon which every durable resistance is built.

AXIOM III

Iraqi Modernist Lineage

إرث الحداثة العراقية

From Jewad Selim’s Monument to Freedom in Tahrir Square to Shakir Hassan Al Said’s contemplative walls, we inherit a tradition where ancient Mesopotamian clay and radical avant-garde form converse as equals.

AXIOM IV

Horizontal Commons

المشاع الأفقي التكافلي

No kings, no masters, only each other. We protect our community libraries, our autonomous chaikhanas, and our children from authoritarian capture through mutual aid and quiet steadfastness.
